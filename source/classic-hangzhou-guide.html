TIMELESS HANGZHOU

Classic Hangzhou: The Essentials

"Heaven above, Suzhou and Hangzhou below." — West Lake, ancient temples, tea villages, and culinary heritage that inspired poets for centuries.

West Lake (西湖)

A living Chinese landscape painting — UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2011

More than just a lake — West Lake is a cultural landscape of causeways, pagodas, gardens, and islands that has inspired Chinese poets and painters for over a thousand years. The classic "Ten Scenes of West Lake" each represent a different season, time of day, or weather condition.

🚶 Walk or Cycle

Start at Broken Bridge → Bai Causeway → Gushan (Solitary Hill) → Xiling Seal Engravers' Society. Or rent a bike and cycle the Su Causeway. Quieter spots: north shore, Maojiabu.

⛵ Boat Cruise

Take a traditional painted boat to Three Pools Mirroring the Moon (Santan Yinyue) — the iconic image on the ¥1 banknote. Gorgeous classic perspectives of the lake.

🏯 Leifeng Pagoda

Modern reconstruction (2002) atop original 10th-century foundations. Escalator to the top for the definitive panoramic view of West Lake. Houses artifacts from the underground palace.

🌸 Best Times

Spring: Peach blossoms on Su Causeway · Summer: Lotus flowers · Autumn: Moon over Three Pools · Winter: Lingering Snow on Broken Bridge

Sacred & Historic Sites

Ancient temples, gardens, and heritage that shaped Hangzhou's identity

Must-Visit ¥45 (Feilai Feng)

Lingyin Temple (灵隐寺)

One of China's oldest and most important Buddhist temples, founded in 326 AD. Hidden among misty peaks northwest of West Lake. The nearby Feilai Feng ("Flying Peak") features hundreds of ancient Buddhist stone carvings in limestone cliffs.

Pro tip: Arrive at 7:30 AM to beat crowds

Time needed: 2–3 hours

Note: Free temple entry but daily visitor limit — register ahead

Serene Nature ¥80

Xixi National Wetland Park

Urban wetland paradise with quiet waterways, lush vegetation, rich birdlife, and preserved rural villages. Take a boat ride through winding waterways. Far from the West Lake crowds — peaceful and wild.

Time needed: Half day

Crowd level: Low on weekdays, moderate weekends

Best for: Nature lovers, bird watching, quiet escape

UNESCO Free (area)

Grand Canal (京杭大运河)

Southern terminus of the world's longest ancient canal (1,794km to Beijing). The Gongchen Bridge area is most atmospheric with Qing-Dynasty stone embankments, canal-side teahouses, and museums (Umbrella Museum, Knife & Sword Museum).

Don't miss: ¥3 water bus from Wulin Gate to Gongchen Bridge

Explore: Xiaohezhi Street historic neighborhood

Also: Canal night cruises available

Historic Street Free

Hefang Street & Southern Song

Historic pedestrian street lined with traditional shops, craft stalls, snack vendors, and traditional Chinese medicine halls. While touristy, it's still the classic old Hangzhou experience. For a more authentic feel, explore nearby Southern Song Imperial Street.

Try: Local snacks, traditional crafts, tea houses

Better alternative: Southern Song Imperial Street nearby

Best time: Evening when lanterns are lit

Hangzhou Cuisine

Delicate, refined flavors from one of China's great culinary traditions

Signature Dish

Dongpo Pork (东坡肉)

Named after poet Su Dongpo. Pork belly braised slowly in Shaoxing rice wine, soy sauce, and rock sugar until the fat turns translucent. Rich, glossy, faintly sweet, and meltingly tender.

Delicate

Longjing Tea Shrimp (龙井虾仁)

Fresh river shrimp stir-fried with young Longjing tea leaves. The iconic Hangzhou dish — clean, delicate, fragrant, and elegant. A must-try at classic restaurants.

Traditional

Beggar's Chicken (叫花鸡)

Whole chicken stuffed with spices, wrapped in lotus leaves, encased in mud, and roasted. The mud is cracked open at the table, revealing incredibly fragrant and tender chicken. Takes ~6 hours to prepare — order ahead!

Classic

West Lake Vinegar Fish

Live carp briefly starved, then cooked and served with a sweet-sour vinegar sauce. Quality varies dramatically by restaurant — stick to established old-brand places like Lou Wai Lou or Zhi Wei Guan.

Noodles

Hangzhou-Style Noodles

Try Fang Lao Da (Michelin Bib Gourmand) for authentic Hangzhou noodles. Or explore Wulin Night Market for street food variety. Pian'er Chuan (sliced pork noodles with bamboo shoots) is a local favorite.

Where to Eat

Classic Restaurants

Lou Wai Lou (est. 1848) — lakeside, iconic, hosted dignitaries. Zhi Wei Guan — balances authenticity and accessibility. Grandma's Kitchen — local favorite chain, excellent value.

Day Trips from Hangzhou

Water towns, lakes, and ancient villages within easy reach

Scenic

Qiandao Lake

Thousand Island Lake — massive man-made reservoir with 1,078 islands. Crystal clear water, scenic cruises, island hopping, and outdoor activities. About 2 hours by high-speed train or bus.

Water Town

Wuzhen / Xitang

Classic Jiangnan water towns with stone bridges, canals, and whitewashed houses. Wuzhen is beautifully restored (more touristy but stunning); Xitang is more atmospheric and authentic. Both ~1 hour by train.

Ancient

Liangzhu & Archaeological Sites

Liangzhu Ancient City ruins — a 5,000-year-old Neolithic jade civilization, UNESCO World Heritage Site. The museum is world-class. Trace the dawn of Chinese civilization just outside the city.
