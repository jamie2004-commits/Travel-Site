TIMELESS SHANGHAI

Classic Shanghai: The Essentials

From the Bund's colonial waterfront to Ming Dynasty gardens, soup dumplings to river cruises — these are the timeless experiences that define Shanghai.

Iconic Landmarks

The world-famous sights that put Shanghai on the map

Most Iconic Free

The Bund (外滩)

Shanghai's signature 1.5km waterfront promenade. 52 historic colonial buildings in Gothic, Baroque, Romanesque, and Art Deco styles face the futuristic Pudong skyline across the Huangpu River.

Best time: Dusk (6:30–9 PM) — watch the city light up

Pro tip: Skip the Bund Sightseeing Tunnel — it's a tourist trap

Quieter spot: North Bund for fewer crowds

Historic ¥40

Yu Garden & Yuyuan Bazaar (豫园)

A 400-year-old Ming Dynasty classical Chinese garden built in 1559. Features exquisite rockeries, koi ponds, pavilions, and the famous Exquisite Jade Rock. The surrounding bazaar offers traditional snacks and crafts.

Hours: 9:00–16:30 (closed Mondays)

Pro tip: Arrive at opening to beat tour groups

Don't miss: Nine-turn Bridge and Lake Pavilion

Skyline ¥180

Shanghai Tower Observation Deck

China's tallest building and the world's 2nd tallest at 632m. The observation deck on the 118th–119th floors offers a breathtaking 360° panoramic view of the entire city from above the clouds.

Time needed: 1–2 hours

Also nearby: Oriental Pearl Tower, Jin Mao Tower, SWFC

Tip: Clear days give the best views

Shopping Free

Nanjing Road (南京路)

China's premier shopping street stretching 5.5km from the Bund to Jing'an. The pedestrian section between the Bund and People's Square buzzes with shoppers, neon signs, historic stores, and modern malls.

Highlights: 600+ businesses, luxury brands, theaters, cafes

West Nanjing Road: Plaza 66, IFC Mall for luxury shopping

Best time: Evening when neon lights are on

Neighborhoods & Culture

Wander historic districts and discover Shanghai's layered heritage

Must Wander

Former French Concession

Tree-lined plane trees, cozy alleyways, hidden coffee shops, boutique stores, and European-style architecture. The most charming neighborhood in Shanghai — perfect for a half-day wandering.

Key streets: Wukang Road, Anfu Road, Changle Road

World-Class Free

Shanghai Museum

China's most important museum of classical Chinese art. Unique round-top, square-base design. Four floors of ancient bronzes, ceramics, jade, calligraphy, paintings, and seals.

Location: People's Square

Ancient Temple ¥50

Jing'an Temple

1,700+ year-old Buddhist temple (est. 247 CE) dramatically contrasting with surrounding modern skyscrapers. Striking golden architecture houses a 3.8m jade Buddha — the largest sitting jade Buddha in China.

Metro: Lines 2, 7, 14 to Jing'an Temple

Sacred ¥20

Jade Buddha Temple

Active Buddhist temple founded in 1882, housing two precious jade Buddha statues brought from Burma. A serene spiritual escape in the city. The vegetarian restaurant here is also highly regarded.

Don't miss: The vegetarian temple cuisine

Modern Art

West Bund Art District

Shanghai's contemporary art hub along the Huangpu River. Former industrial spaces converted into galleries, museums, and creative spaces. Great riverside walking path too.

Highlights: Long Museum, Yuz Museum, West Bund Art Center

Art Deco

Peace Hotel & Fairmont

Iconic Art Deco landmark on the Bund since 1929. Step into the grand lobby for a taste of old Shanghai glamour, or enjoy afternoon tea. The rooftop offers incredible Bund views.

Vibe: Old Shanghai elegance, jazz history

Nighttime & Entertainment

Shanghai truly comes alive after dark — cruises, shows, and skyline views

Evening Essential ~¥120–200

Huangpu River Night Cruise

See Shanghai from the water! Glide past the illuminated Bund on one side and the glowing Pudong skyscrapers on the other. A classic 1-hour cruise that combines the city's past and future.

Departs from: Shiliupu Pier

Duration: ~1 hour

Best time: After dark when all lights are on

Cultural Show ~¥180–580

ERA Acrobatics Show

A breathtaking fusion of traditional Chinese acrobatics and modern stage effects. Incredible balancing, juggling, and contortion acts that appeal to all ages — no language barrier!

Show time: Usually 7:30–9:00 PM

Often paired with: Huangpu River cruise combo tickets

Perfect for: Families, first-time visitors

Iconic ~¥150/drink

Rooftop Bars on the Bund

Sip cocktails with jaw-dropping views of the Pudong skyline. The Bund has a walkable cluster of rooftop bars — perfect for bar-hopping.

Roosevelt Sky Bar: Featured in "Blossoms Shanghai" drama

Bar Rouge: Lively, famous Thursday Burlesque Nights

Sir Elly's Terrace (Peninsula): Ultra-luxurious

WET Bar (W Hotel): Relaxed vibe, fewer crowds

Live Music Varies

Jazz Clubs & Live Music

Shanghai has a vibrant live music scene, especially for jazz. The city's jazz heritage dates back to the 1920s.

JZ Club: Legendary jazz club on Hengshan Road

Peace Hotel Jazz Bar: Classic old Shanghai jazz

Asia Mansion: 20+ small theaters in one building

Found 158: Underground nightlife complex near Jing'an

Food Experiences

Taste your way through Shanghai's culinary heritage

Xiaolongbao (小笼包) — Soup Dumplings

The iconic Shanghai dumpling — delicate paper-thin skins filled with seasoned pork and rich, hot gelatinous broth. How to eat: pick gently with chopsticks, nibble a small hole, sip the broth first, then eat the dumpling (with vinegar and ginger).

Jia Jia Tang Bao

Near People's Square. Locals' favorite. Crab roe version is legendary. Silky-thin skin, powerful stock. ~¥28–40.

Nanxiang Steamed Bun

Yu Garden area. Historic spot since 1900. Authentic experience, can have long queues. The classic.

Din Tai Fung

Multiple locations. Internationally recognized, consistent quality, clean modern setting. Great for beginners.

Must Try

Shengjian Bao (生煎包)

Pan-fried pork buns — crispy bottom, soft top, juicy inside. Shanghai's other signature dumpling. Look for street stalls and local shops.

Nostalgic

Shanghai Fried Pork Cutlet

Crispy outside, juicy inside. A nostalgic local favorite loved by generations. Often served with a special Worcestershire-style sauce.

Food Tour

French Concession Food Walk

Evening walking food tours through the French Concession with a local guide. Discover family-run eateries, scallion noodles, wontons, sesame sauce dumplings, and more.

Seasonal

Hairy Crab (Oct–Dec)

Shanghai's autumn obsession. Chinese mitten crabs from Yangcheng Lake, prized for their rich roe. A seasonal culinary splurge you'll never forget.
