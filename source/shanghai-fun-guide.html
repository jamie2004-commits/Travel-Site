SHANGHAI CITY GUIDE

Fun Things to Do in Shanghai

From heart-pounding escape rooms to high-speed go-karting, discover the most thrilling and entertaining experiences China's most vibrant city has to offer.

Top Escape Rooms

Shanghai has some of the best escape rooms in the world — immersive storytelling, intricate puzzles, and cinematic production

#1 Ranked ~¥180

魔方剧情密室 (Cube Story)

Shanghai's undisputed #1 escape room since 2012. The pioneer of story-driven escape rooms with professional production quality and multiple locations.

Story-Driven Puzzles

Location: Xuhui District & multiple branches

#2 Ranked ~¥160

屋有岛机械剧情密室

Renowned for exquisitely crafted mechanical puzzles and complete story design. A consistent top-3 favorite in Shanghai's escape room community.

Mechanical Puzzles

Location: Xuhui District

Immersive Art Premium

UMEPLAY 逃脱艺术

Cinematic escape art experience combining film-level storytelling with interactive theater. The "Afterlife" theme is legendary — sensory-driven journey with wireless headphone translation available.

Cinematic Theatrical

Location: Moganshan Road, Putuo District

Horror ~¥200

怪谈屋真人鬼屋密室

Shanghai's premier horror-themed escape room featuring touchable live NPC actors. Not for the faint-hearted — one of the most popular horror experiences since 2017.

Horror Live Actors

Vibe: Scary, intense, interactive NPCs

Budget Friendly ~¥110

MC 精品真人密室逃脱

One of Shanghai's original escape room brands since 2012. Great value with 4 locations across the city including Pudong. First to bring escape rooms into shopping malls.

Classic Value

Locations: 4 branches citywide

Comedy Varies

Storm Island Immersive Center

Hilarious interactive escape room experience where you play as a village chief. Thoughtful set design, great costumes and props, rich storyline that leaves a lasting impression.

Funny Roleplay

Location: Bailian Century Shopping Center, Pudong

Go-Karting Venues

From professional F1-grade tracks to rooftop sunset racing — get your adrenaline fix

Pro Level ¥200+/8min

Shanghai International Circuit Kart Land

The ultimate karting destination — located inside the actual F1 circuit! A professional-grade 1.234km track with 18 challenging corners, CIK/FIA standards, and 390cc karts reaching ~80km/h.

Outdoor F1 Venue High Speed

Hours: Tue–Sun 9:00–17:00 (closed Mon)

Location: 2000 Yining Road, Jiading District

Tip: Go on a dry weekday for fewer crowds and max laps

Indoor/Outdoor Varies

Peak Racing (极烽赛车)

Unique dual-track design that transitions seamlessly between indoor and outdoor sections. Nearly 1km track perfect for rainy days. Free racing suit trials and on-site cafe.

All-Weather Photogenic

Location: 195 Puhui Road, Songjiang District

Best for: Racing in any weather, great photos

Technical ¥266/hr

Mosu Karting (魔速卡丁车)

Designed by professional racer Ma Qinghua. Multi-level indoor track with tight Monaco-style rhythm, 20+ curves, and an exciting 2F-to-1F elevation drop. Weekday unlimited play available.

Indoor Multi-Level

Location: Near Yunjin Road Metro (Line 11), Xuhui

Note: Must log sub-1min lap to unlock Pro karts

Sunset Views 7-lap sessions

Caolu Rooftop Karting

Rooftop karting on the 6th floor of a shopping mall! Western US wilderness meets sci-fi theme. Offers single, double-seat, and kids' karts. Catch incredible sunset views while racing.

Rooftop Family Friendly Scenic

Location: 99 Guatong Road, 6F, Pudong

Best for: Couples, families, sunset photos

Central Pudong Varies

Lisu Racing Park (砺速卡丁车)

Pro competitive outdoor karting inside the Middle Ring Road in Pudong — super convenient location! Two track layouts (forward & reverse) with 13 challenging turns. FS200cc adult karts available.

Outdoor Great Value

Location: Near Yuntai Road Station (Lines 6 & 7), Pudong

Best for: Competitive racing, central location

Off-Road Adventure

Wild Karting (荒野卡丁车)

A wild 3.3km natural wilderness trail featuring floating bridges, mud roads, creeks, and slopes. Real off-road adventure through jungle-like terrain. A completely different karting experience!

Dirt Track Adventure

Track: 3.3km natural wilderness trail

Best for: Thrill-seekers who want something different

Pro Tips for First-Time Karting

Book ahead: Popular venues can have 2+ hour waits on weekends
Safety first: Remove all jewelry before your helmet goes on
Single-seater: Best for your first try — doubles are slower and heavier
Don't full-throttle: Complex corners need lifting off or braking
Stalled? Stay put: Wave both hands to alert marshals — don't exit the kart
Photo time: Visit outdoor venues 3–4 PM for golden sunset lighting

More Fun Things to Do

VR experiences, immersive theater, bowling, archery, and other unique activities

Immersive Theater

Sleep No More

World-famous immersive theater experience at the McKinnon Hotel. Inspired by Macbeth — wear a white mask and roam freely through a mysterious multi-floor film-noir world.

VR Experience

PARTYDAY Super Star Carnival

Massive 8,500m² entertainment complex with 80+ projects including DOF-VR Robotic Arm with 360° rotation — feel roller coaster weightlessness and racing thrills!

Bowling

Party King Bowling Bar

Shanghai's first hidden pink bowling alley! 10 lanes with pink & purple neon lights — like bowling at an electronic party. Also has bar, coffee, and snacks.

Archery

Fantasy City Archery Range

Immersive archery with panoramic projection. Plus board games, billiards, darts, arcade games, KTV, drum machine, and boxing machine — one-stop fun!

1990s Theme

New World: Golden Age

Faithfully recreates 1990s Huanghe Road atmosphere. Real film set + interactive gameplay with sensor wristbands, 18 checkpoints, trivia challenges, and bartending games.

VR Immersive

Qinchao Awakening VR

Qin Dynasty culture VR immersive exploration. Like a sci-fi historical drama — modern archaeologists discover Qin Shi Huang's mausoleum and a mysterious underground world.

Mixed Reality

TAIXU Dreamscape

World's first mixed reality immersive theater using Apple Vision Pro headsets. Navigate a 400m² physical set while experiencing a tragic romance through MR lenses.

Classic Bowling

Orden Bowling Centre

Classic bowling alley on Hengshan Road with 16 well-maintained lanes, plus billiard hall and game arcade. A reliable central option for a fun night out.

VR Theme Park

Joypolis / VR Zones

Cyberpunk-themed VR playgrounds with free-roaming shooters and cinematic experiences. Futuristic neon interiors make for great photos before you even put on a headset.
