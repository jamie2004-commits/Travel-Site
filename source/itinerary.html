出发夜
Thursday · Departure
Red eye
  1. 20:30
    Leave for the airport
    Aim to be at check in three hours before a 23:45 departure. Eat a proper dinner before you go, the flight meal will land at an odd hour.
  2. 23:45
    Flight departs for 上海浦东 Shanghai Pudong
    Roughly 5.5 hours. Sleep on board if you can, day one starts at 05:15 with no hotel room until noon.
Pack for this trip
  • Wedding outfit packed flat and accessible, you change on day two.
  • Late September in both cities: 20 to 28 degrees, warm days, cool evenings by the lake. Bring one light layer.
  • Comfortable shoes. West Lake, the tea fields and Disney are all long walking days.
  • Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay with an overseas card before you fly. Cash is awkward almost everywhere now.
  • Download offline maps and a VPN if you use one.

抵达 · 西湖日落
Friday · Arrive and walk West Lake
Long day
  1. 05:15
    Land at 浦东国际机场 Pudong
    Immigration and bags will take 45 to 60 minutes at that hour.
  2. 06:30
    Airport breakfast
    Congee, wonton noodles or a fast food set in the arrivals area. Nothing heavy, you have a long bus ride next.
    ¥30 to 60 per person
  3. 08:50
    Airport coach to 杭州 Hangzhou
    Direct long distance coach from Pudong, roughly 3.5 to 4 hours depending on traffic. Buy tickets at the counter in the arrivals hall or on the operator app. Backup option if the coach is full: Maglev or Metro Line 2 to Hongqiao, then high speed rail to Hangzhou East, about one hour of train time.
    Coach ¥100 to 130 · Rail backup ¥75 to 95
  4. 12:30
    Hotel check in
    Email the hotel now to ask for an early check in, you will arrive tired and the standard time is noon. If the room is not ready, leave bags and eat first.
  5. 13:00
    Lunch near the hotel
    Keep it local and quick. A bowl of 片儿川 noodles or a small Hangzhou set is the right size before a nap.
    ¥40 to 80 per person
  6. 14:00
    Nap
    Set an alarm. Two hours maximum or the evening is gone.
  7. 16:00
    Out to 西湖 West Lake
    Sunset falls around 18:03 to 18:07. Walk the 白堤 Bai Causeway toward 断桥 Broken Bridge, then down the lakeside promenade. Optional additions: a hand rowed boat, or 雷峰塔 Leifeng Pagoda for the sunset view over the water. Get to your sunset spot by 17:30, the good stretches fill up.
    Lake walk free · Boat ¥55 to 70 · Pagoda ¥40
  8. 18:45
    Dinner near 湖滨 Hubin
    The lakeside mall strip has plenty of options and is a five minute walk from the promenade. Try 东坡肉 braised pork, 龙井虾仁 shrimp with Longjing tea, or 西湖醋鱼 if you are feeling brave, it is divisive.
    ¥60 to 130 per person
  9. 20:30
    Back to the hotel, early night
    Tomorrow is the wedding. Bank the sleep.

20 / 09
龙井茶山
Sunday · Tea plantation and group dinner
Group day
  1. 09:30
    Check out of the resort
    Move luggage back to the Zhejiang Hotel and check in again, or leave bags at the front desk if the room is not ready.
    Taxi ¥80 to 200
  2. 11:00
    Tea plantation day with the group
    Most likely 龙井村 Longjing Village or 梅家坞 Meijiawu. Walk the terraces, sit at a farmhouse tea house, and drink a pot outdoors. September is not harvest season, so it is quiet and green rather than busy. If anyone wants to buy tea, buy from a farmhouse rather than a roadside stall, and expect to be offered several grades.
    Tea house ¥50 to 150 · Tea to bring home ¥200 up
  3. 13:00
    Lunch at a 农家乐 farmhouse restaurant
    These are all over the tea villages. Order family style with the group, it works out cheaper and you taste more.
    ¥70 to 130 per person
  4. 17:30
    Group dinner
    Worth joining. Bigger table means more dishes and a better spread, and it is easier than finding somewhere on your own.
    ¥80 to 180 per person
  5. 19:30
    Shopping at 湖滨银泰 in77
    Lakeside mall cluster, open late, with a musical fountain on the lake side in the evening. Good for last minute anything and a decent dessert stop.

21 / 09
灵隐寺 · 转场上海
Monday · Lingyin Temple, then move to Shanghai
Transfer day
  1. 08:00
    Breakfast and pack
    Check out and leave luggage at the desk so you are not dragging it to the temple.
  2. 09:00
    灵隐寺 Lingyin Temple, optional
    Two tickets: one for 飞来峰 Feilai Peak scenic area and a second for the temple itself, bought inside. Go early, it is calmest before 10:00. Allow two hours including the carved grottoes along the walk in. Skip it without guilt if everyone is tired, you have a train to catch.
    ¥45 scenic area + ¥30 temple
  3. 12:00
    Lunch, then collect bags
    The vegetarian noodles at the temple canteen are cheap and surprisingly good if you are still up there.
    ¥25 to 70 per person
  4. 14:00
    High speed rail 杭州东 to 上海虹桥
    Around one hour, departures every 15 to 30 minutes. Book seats a few days ahead for a Monday afternoon. Then Metro Line 2 across to Lujiazui, roughly 45 minutes, or a taxi for about 40 minutes.
    Rail ¥73 to 95 · Metro ¥7 · Taxi ¥120 to 160
  5. 16:30
    Check in, drop bags, head out
    You are on the Pudong riverside, which puts the skyline cluster and the ferry to the Bund within easy reach.
  6. 17:30
    Activity one: 陆家嘴 Lujiazui skyline
    Either go up 上海中心 Shanghai Tower for the highest view, or stay at ground level and walk the riverside. Book tower tickets online for a timed slot near sunset.
    Tower ¥180 · Riverside walk free
  7. 19:00
    Dinner
    Local 本帮菜 Shanghainese if you want the classics: 红烧肉 braised pork belly, 生煎 pan fried buns, 小笼包 soup dumplings.
    ¥70 to 150 per person
  8. 20:30
    Activity two: 外滩 the Bund at night
    Take the 轮渡 ferry across from Pudong for a couple of yuan, it is the cheapest skyline cruise in the city. The Bund facade lights usually stay on until around 22:00 to 23:00, so do not leave it too late.
    Ferry ¥2 · Waterfront free

22 / 09
上海漫游
Tuesday · Slow city day
Flexible
  1. 09:30
    Sleep in, slow start
    Your only unstructured day. Do not over plan it, Disney tomorrow is brutal on the legs.
  2. 12:00
    Lunch: a Michelin listed room
    Lunch sets at starred and Bib Gourmand places in Shanghai are far cheaper than dinner for nearly the same kitchen. Book one or two weeks ahead for the starred ones. Bib Gourmand noodle and dumpling shops need no booking at all and are the better value.
    Bib Gourmand ¥60 to 150 · One star lunch ¥300 to 700
  3. 14:00
    Activity three: 武康路 Wukang Road and the former French Concession
    Plane tree lined streets, low rise villas, coffee shops and small boutiques. The best walking in Shanghai and it costs nothing. Wukang Mansion at the fork is the photo everyone takes.
    Free · coffee ¥30 to 55
  4. 16:30
    Activity four: pick one
    豫园 Yu Garden and the old town bazaar for classical gardens and snack stalls, garden entry around ¥40. Or 田子坊 Tianzifang for lane alleys and craft shops, free. Or 南京东路 Nanjing East Road if the group wants straightforward shopping.
    ¥0 to 40
  5. 18:30
    Dinner: 串串香 skewer hotpot
    The skewer hotpot idea from your draft fits here nicely. You pick sticks from a fridge, cook them at the table, and pay by the stick, so the bill is whatever you make it. Lively and good for a group.
    ¥80 to 160 per person
  6. 20:30
    Activity five: massage, or the Bund if you missed it
    A 60 to 90 minute foot and shoulder massage is the correct choice the night before a theme park. Chains near Lujiazui are clean and reliable.
    ¥150 to 320 for 60 to 90 minutes

23 / 09
上海迪士尼
Wednesday · Disneyland, all day
Full day out
  1. 06:45
    Early wake, breakfast, go
    Metro Line 11 runs direct to Disney Resort station. From Lujiazui allow about an hour door to gate. Being at the gate before opening is worth two extra rides.
    Metro ¥6 to 9
  2. 08:00
    Park entry
    Tickets are dated and priced by demand tier, so a late September midweek day usually sits in a mid tier. Buy in advance through the official channel, gate prices and availability are worse. Add the paid queue skip only if the app shows long waits, it is priced per ride.
    Ticket ¥475 to 700 · Queue skip ¥80 up per ride
  3. Through the day
    Food inside the park
    The beef noodle soup you mentioned is worth chasing down, confirm which restaurant with the couple before you go. Park food runs ¥60 to ¥120 a meal. Bring your own sealed water, it saves a fortune.
    ¥120 to 250 per person for the day
  4. 20:00
    Evening castle show and fireworks
    Grab a viewing spot about 30 minutes early. Expect the exit crush afterwards, and expect the metro to be packed.
  5. 22:00
    Back to the hotel
    Start packing tonight. Tomorrow morning is tight.
