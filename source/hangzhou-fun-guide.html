HANGZHOU CITY GUIDE

Fun Things to Do in Hangzhou

Beyond the iconic West Lake — discover Hangzhou's best escape rooms, go-karting tracks, and thrilling entertainment experiences.

Top Escape Rooms

Hangzhou's immersive puzzle experiences — from sci-fi thrillers to Southeast Asian horror

Next-Gen Varies

暴风岛次时代密室 (Storm Island Next-Gen Escape Rooms)

Next-gen escape room at Huanglong International Center K-lab. Original themes: sci-fi mystery "Deep Sea Echo," post-apocalyptic "Doomsday Ark," and Chinese folk horror "Paper Bride." 4-8 players, recommended 6 for full experience.

Sci-Fi Horror Story-Driven

Location: Huanglong, Xihu District

Immersive Horror Premium

幻觉沉浸式剧场 (Hallucination Immersive Theatre)

Southeast Asian-themed horror "Road to the Underworld: Gluttony." Spacious scenes with cinema-level sound and light effects, outstanding NPC acting, intense chase sequences, and clever mechanisms. Even has wheelchair-accessible spots!

Horror Live NPCs

Location: Xintiandi, Gongshu District

World-Class Show

宋城 · 千古情 (Songcheng Romance Show)

Songcheng theme park with "The Romantic Show of Songcheng" — hailed as one of the "World's Top 3 Must-See Shows" alongside Las Vegas' "O" and Paris' Moulin Rouge. Epic sound, lighting, and stage mechanics bring ancient Liangzhu to life.

Vibe: Cultural theme park + spectacular show

Water Spectacle

印象西湖 · 最忆是杭州 (Impression West Lake)

"Enduring Memories of Hangzhou" — open-air spectacle directed by Zhang Yimou (2008 Olympics ceremony director). Stage is submerged in West Lake itself. Dance, opera, and music against the lake backdrop. Book 3+ days in advance.

Time: 19:40 daily · ~55–66 min

Nature Adventure

垂云通天河 (Chuiyun Underground River)

In Tonglu — vertical underground river with crystal clear water and natural stalactites. Features NPC interactions,地心探险 (Earth's Core Adventure) with water guns through stone caves, plus glass skywalk and mountain karting.

Location: Tonglu County · 1.5hr from Hangzhou

Multi-Activity

红博苑户外营地 (Hongboyuan Outdoor Camp)

Xiaoshan outdoor complex with 6 activities: karting, BBQ camping, paintball/CQB, kayaking, archery, and fruit/vegetable picking. Perfect for large groups, team building, or a full day of outdoor fun.

Location: Xiaoshan District

Tea Experience

Longjing Tea Village

Walk through terraced tea fields, learn about China's most famous green tea, watch traditional pan-roasting, and sip fresh tea at a local farmer's home. Best March–May, especially pre-Qingming (early April) for premium leaves.

Location: Meijiawu / Longjing villages

Canal Cruise

Grand Canal Water Bus

Ride the public water bus from Wulin Gate to Gongchen Bridge for only ~¥3! A unique perspective on Hangzhou's residential life along the UNESCO World Heritage Grand Canal. Explore Xiaohezhi Street and canal museums at the end.

Best for: Budget-friendly local experience

Classic Hangzhou

The old streets, walks and heritage sites locals actually rate, with a straight verdict on each

MUST GO Free

小河直街 (Xiaohe Straight Street)

Hangzhou's own old quarter on the Grand Canal, and the one that never got scrubbed into a souvenir street. Whitewashed walls, black tile roofs, people still living in them, and a row of canal-side coffee roasters, craft studios and teahouses. The local pick for a slow afternoon walk and the best Jiangnan photographs in the city.

Canal Coffee and Teahouses Citywalk

Location: Xiaohe Straight Street, Gongshu District, on the Grand Canal

Time needed: 2 hours

WORTH A DETOUR Free

桥西历史街区 (Qiaoxi Historic Block)

The other bank of the Gongchen Bridge, five minutes from Xiaohe Straight Street: restored Qing warehouses now holding the Umbrella, Fan and Knife and Sword museums, and the street food stalls locals queue at. Pair the two in one walk rather than making a separate trip.

Canal Museums Street Food

Location: Qiaoxi, Gongchen Bridge, Grand Canal, Gongshu District

Time needed: 1-2 hours

MUST GO Free

九溪十八涧 (Nine Creeks and Eighteen Gullies)

The walk everyone in Hangzhou does and almost no visitor finds: a Y-shaped valley of small streams running down through tea terraces and forest to the Qiantang River. Cool in summer, red leaves in late autumn, and mist through the trees the morning after rain. Flat, shaded, and about 5km end to end.

Walk Tea Terraces Free

Location: West Lake scenic area, Xihu District, below Longjing

Time needed: 3 hours

WORTH A DETOUR ¥8

云栖竹径 (Yunqi Bamboo Path)

A stone path through a dense bamboo grove on the far side of the hills, several degrees cooler than the lake and almost silent on a weekday. Small, quick, and the obvious pairing with Nine Creeks or Meijiawu since all three sit on the same road.

Bamboo Quiet Summer Escape

Location: Yunqi Road, West Lake scenic area, Xihu District

Time needed: 1 hour

MUST GO Free

良渚古城遗址公园 (Liangzhu Ancient City Ruins Park)

UNESCO World Heritage and the source of 5,000-year-old Liangzhu civilisation, on a site big enough to need its own shuttle. Mojiaoshan palace mound, the Fanshan royal tombs, a deer park you can feed, and the jade in the Liangzhu Museum nearby. Free, but book the entry slot ahead online.

UNESCO Free Booking Half Day

Location: Yuhang, north-west of the city, about 40 minutes out on Metro Line 2

Time needed: Half day

MUST GO Free

南宋德寿宫遗址博物馆 (Deshou Palace Museum)

The Southern Song imperial palace excavated in place and roofed over, with the foundations left exactly as dug and the hall rebuilt in projection above them. The best single hour on why Hangzhou was once the capital of China. Free, timed entry, and closed Mondays.

Southern Song Indoor Rainy Day

Location: Wangjiang Road near Qingtai Street, Shangcheng District

Time needed: 1-2 hours

WORTH A DETOUR ¥20

六和塔 (Liuhe Pagoda)

A 970 AD pagoda built to calm the Qiantang tidal bore, thirteen exterior storeys up the hillside with the river and the bridge laid out below. Worth climbing if you are already heading out this way, not worth a special trip in from the lake.

Pagoda River View Climb

Location: Zhijiang Road, West Lake District, above the Qiantang River

Time needed: 1 hour

WORTH A DETOUR Free

太子湾公园 (Taiziwan Park)

The lakeside park that goes viral every spring for cherry blossom and tulips, and is an ordinary pleasant park the rest of the year. Come in the March to April flowering window or skip it: at peak bloom it is shoulder to shoulder, and out of season there is nothing here the lake shore does not already give you.

Spring Only Blossom Free

Location: Nanshan Road, south of West Lake, Xihu District

Time needed: 1 hour

WORTH A DETOUR Free

满觉陇 (Manjuelong Osmanthus Valley)

A valley of osmanthus trees behind the lake that the whole city smells for about three weeks each autumn. Teahouses put tables out under the trees and you sit in the falling flowers. Late September to October only, and genuinely one of the best things in Hangzhou in that window.

Autumn Only Osmanthus Teahouses

Location: Manjuelong Road, south of West Lake, Xihu District

Time needed: 2 hours
