HANGZHOU CITY GUIDE

Fun Things to Do in Hangzhou

Beyond the iconic West Lake — discover Hangzhou's best escape rooms, go-karting tracks, and thrilling entertainment experiences.

Top Escape Rooms

Hangzhou's immersive puzzle experiences — from sci-fi thrillers to Southeast Asian horror

Next-Gen Varies

暴风岛次时代密室

Next-gen escape room at Huanglong International Center K-lab. Original themes: sci-fi mystery "Deep Sea Echo," post-apocalyptic "Doomsday Ark," and Chinese folk horror "Paper Bride." 4-8 players, recommended 6 for full experience.

Sci-Fi Horror Story-Driven

Location: Huanglong, Xihu District

Immersive Horror Premium

幻觉沉浸式剧场

Southeast Asian-themed horror "Road to the Underworld: Gluttony." Spacious scenes with cinema-level sound and light effects, outstanding NPC acting, intense chase sequences, and clever mechanisms. Even has wheelchair-accessible spots!

Horror Live NPCs

Location: Xintiandi, Gongshu District

VR Immersive Varies

VR Escape Rooms (金沙印象城)

Immersive VR game escape rooms at Jinsha Impression City. 360° three-dimensional thrills with multiple themes available. Some combine real sets with VR for the ultimate experience. Great for tech-savvy thrill-seekers.

VR Futuristic

Location: Jinsha Impression City, Qiantang District

Great Value ~¥90

OMG剧情密室逃脱

Local Hangzhou brand with two branches and six themes. Well-regarded for logical puzzle design and rich variety of themes. A solid choice for quality escape rooms at a budget-friendly price point.

Variety Value

Location: West Lake Culture Plaza

Variety ~¥140

GY-BOX密室逃脱

Highly-rated escape room in Linping New World. Open until midnight. Good variety of themes and quality production. Popular among locals for weekend group outings and team building.

Late Hours Groups

Hours: 10:00–00:00 · Linping District

Chain Brand Varies

Xcape密室逃脱

Well-known chain brand with multiple creative themes including "Wizard of Oz" fantasy adventure. 6-9 players recommended for the full experience. Reliable quality and varied difficulty levels.

Fantasy Reliable

Best for: Groups of friends, varied themes

Go-Karting Venues

Indoor tracks, outdoor circuits, and even mountain karting adventures

Central Location Varies

量子空间竞技主题乐园

Massive indoor competitive theme park near Hangzhou East Station. Huge closed-loop indoor track with rich curves, beginner-friendly. No need to worry about sun or rain. Beyond karting: also offers VR, climbing, and more.

Location: Near Hangzhou East Station (900m walk from Exit B)

Hours: Weekdays 12:30–19:00 · Weekends 11:00–20:00

Vibe: Tech-feel track, great for drifting

Indoor Pro Varies

F2万奥赛车城

Professional indoor karting venue in Gongshu District at Hangzhou Auto City. High-spec track design with professional kart equipment. One of the largest indoor karting tracks in Hangzhou's main urban area.

Location: 5th Floor, Auto City C Hall, Gongshu District

Best for: Beginners & advanced players alike

Also: Billiards, arcade games on-site

Mall-Based Varies

星耀7号卡丁车 (吾悦广场店)

Indoor karting inside Gongshu Wuyue Plaza shopping mall. Part of a chain brand with standardized operations. Great for casual family outings, beginners, and light competitive play. Easy to combine with shopping and dining.

Location: Wuyue Plaza, Gongshu District

Best for: Families, beginners, casual racing

Outdoor Varies

杭州迪赛卡丁车

Outdoor track built alongside the Xingfu River. Natural breeze in summer makes it more pleasant than air-conditioned indoor venues. 200cc-270cc gasoline karts with longer straights than most indoor tracks — you'll feel real acceleration.

Engine: 200cc-270cc gasoline karts

Vibe: Outdoor riverside, natural wind

Best for: Speed enthusiasts who want fresh air

Scenic Varies

千岛湖燃擎卡丁车俱乐部

Professional karting in the Qiandao Lake (Thousand Island Lake) area. Perfect if you're making a day trip to the lake. Combines scenic tourism with exciting kart racing — great for families, friends, and team building.

Location: Chun'an County, Qiandao Lake area

Best for: Day trips to Thousand Island Lake

Trip tip: Combine with lake cruise and island hopping

Multi-Activity Varies

PARTYDAY运动超乐场 (奥体店)

Massive 12,000m² sports theme park at Olympic Sports Center with 40+ projects! Features the first large indoor multi-level karting track (2,000m²+, 300m+ track). Plus rock climbing, ice skating, and more retro-futuristic fun.

Location: Olympic Sports Center, Xiaoshan District

Karting: Indoor multi-level track, 300m+

Also: Climbing, ice skating, 40+ activities total

Adventure Bonus: Mountain Karting in Tonglu

For the ultimate adventure: bamboo forest drifting + mountain karting at Tonglu Shanye Zhigu (山野之谷). 1.5 hours drive from Hangzhou East Station (or direct shuttle bus ¥60/person). The last 3km is winding mountain road — drive carefully!

Best for: Thrill-seekers wanting something beyond standard tracks

More Fun & Entertainment

Shows, theme parks, and unique Hangzhou experiences

World-Class Show

宋城 · 千古情

Songcheng theme park with "The Romantic Show of Songcheng" — hailed as one of the "World's Top 3 Must-See Shows" alongside Las Vegas' "O" and Paris' Moulin Rouge. Epic sound, lighting, and stage mechanics bring ancient Liangzhu to life.

Vibe: Cultural theme park + spectacular show

Water Spectacle

印象西湖 · 最忆是杭州

"Enduring Memories of Hangzhou" — open-air spectacle directed by Zhang Yimou (2008 Olympics ceremony director). Stage is submerged in West Lake itself. Dance, opera, and music against the lake backdrop. Book 3+ days in advance.

Time: 19:40 daily · ~55–66 min

Nature Adventure

垂云通天河

In Tonglu — vertical underground river with crystal clear water and natural stalactites. Features NPC interactions,地心探险 (Earth's Core Adventure) with water guns through stone caves, plus glass skywalk and mountain karting.

Location: Tonglu County · 1.5hr from Hangzhou

Multi-Activity

红博苑户外营地

Xiaoshan outdoor complex with 6 activities: karting, BBQ camping, paintball/CQB, kayaking, archery, and fruit/vegetable picking. Perfect for large groups, team building, or a full day of outdoor fun.

Location: Xiaoshan District

Tea Experience

Longjing Tea Village

Walk through terraced tea fields, learn about China's most famous green tea, watch traditional pan-roasting, and sip fresh tea at a local farmer's home. Best March–May, especially pre-Qingming (early April) for premium leaves.

Location: Meijiawu / Longjing villages

Canal Cruise

Grand Canal Water Bus

Ride the public water bus from Wulin Gate to Gongchen Bridge for only ~¥3! A unique perspective on Hangzhou's residential life along the UNESCO World Heritage Grand Canal. Explore Xiaohezhi Street and canal museums at the end.

Best for: Budget-friendly local experience
